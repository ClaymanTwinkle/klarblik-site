Privacy policy – Klarblik

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Short version: Klarblik has no account, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect any personal data. Photos, lists and history stay on your phone.

Who is responsible

Klarblik is made by Qitian Chen, an individual developer, who is responsible (the data controller) for the personal data described here. In practice that is only emails you send us. Contact: claymantwinkle@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Ingredient photos and images you pick are read with Apple’s on-device text recognition and are never uploaded. Your avoid lists, family profiles and scan history are stored only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

What is sent over the internet

The app has no server of its own. It connects to two outside services, and you can turn both off in Settings (“Look up on Open Food Facts”):

  • Open Food Facts: when a scanned product is not in the database on your phone, the barcode number (the number of the product, not information about you) is sent to the Open Food Facts public database. Product pictures are also loaded from Open Food Facts. No account, location or device identifier is sent. See the Open Food Facts privacy policy.
  • GitHub: once a week the app downloads the latest product database as a file from GitHub. Nothing is sent except the download request. See the GitHub privacy statement.
Like any internet request, these services can see your IP address. We do not receive it.

Purchases

The one-time purchase goes through Apple’s App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details.

No tracking

There is no advertising, no analytics and no attribution software in the app, and nothing is shared with data brokers.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and message through Gmail (Google). We use them only to answer you, and delete the conversation when the matter is settled, at the latest after 12 months. Ask us at any time and we delete it straight away. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in being able to answer you (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

Your rights

The app gives us no personal data about you, so apart from emails you send us there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can delete everything the app has stored by deleting the app. If you think we handle your personal data wrongly, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live, for example Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY) in Sweden or Datatilsynet in Denmark.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question by email: claymantwinkle@gmail.com, or on GitHub: https://github.com/ClaymanTwinkle/klarblik-site/issues (public). Please do not include health information you do not need to share.

Product data: Open Food Facts (ODbL). Additive names: official EU texts.
