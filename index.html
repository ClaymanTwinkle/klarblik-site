Klarblik support

Klarblik shows the additives, allergens and signs of ultra-processing in food sold in Sweden, Denmark and Finland. Scan the barcode or photograph the ingredient list.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings → Unlock personal features → Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought with. Family members in your Family Sharing group get the purchase automatically.

Will the price change?

What you buy is yours to keep: features you have unlocked stay unlocked, and if the price ever changes, it only applies to new purchases. Scanning, and all information about additives, allergens and processing, will stay free and unlimited.

The result doesn’t match the package.

Tap “Different from the pack?” at the bottom of the result and photograph the ingredient list. The photo is read on your phone, and the result follows what is printed on the package you are holding.

Why is the product not found?

Over 18,000 Nordic products are built into the app and updated weekly. If a product is missing and online lookup is on, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts. If it is not there either, photograph the ingredient list.

Where do the explanations come from?

Every statement points to an official source: EU regulations, EFSA scientific opinions and IARC classifications. The scoring method is public and versioned in the app. The app provides information, not medical advice. Always read the package if you have an allergy.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. The product database and reading of photos work without a connection. Online lookup and the weekly database update can be turned off in Settings.
